■ 01 / CAPITAL MARKET
 
▸ Dhaka stocks slip for third day as energy crisis, global risks weigh
THE BUSINESS STANDARD
DSEX fell 19 points (-0.34%) to close at 5,784 on 26 Jul, a third straight losing session that pushed DSE market cap below Tk7 lakh crore for the first time in weeks, as gas-supply disruption and global geopolitical risk weighed on sentiment. Turnover fell 17% to Tk780cr.
■ 04 / MONEY MARKET & POLICY
 
▸ NBR targets over 10,000 firms to plug Tk38,000cr VAT leakages
THE BUSINESS STANDARD
NBR plans to bring 10,000+ high-risk VAT-registered businesses under enforcement to recover an additional Tk38,000cr in FY27, part of a Tk99,000cr additional-revenue push toward its record Tk6.04 lakh crore collection target for the year.
■ 05 / REGULATORY
 
▸ Share market manipulation: Probe report submission deferred to 23 Sept in case against Shakib, 14 others
THE BUSINESS STANDARD
A Dhaka court set 23 September for the ACC's probe report in the market-manipulation, fraud and money-laundering case against Shakib Al Hasan and 14 others, after the ACC missed today's deadline. The complaint alleges investors lost a combined Tk256.97cr through BO-account manipulation and rumour-mongering.
■ 06 / SECTOR NEWS
 
▸ CNG station owners withdraw 30 July strike amid gas supply crisis
THE BUSINESS STANDARD FUELPOWER
CNG filling-station owners withdrew a planned 30 July symbolic shutdown after a fire at the Maheshkhali LNG terminal further disrupted gas supply, but warned of an indefinite nationwide strike from 23 August unless the sales commission is raised to Tk13.96/m3 from Tk8, unchanged since 2015.
