■ 01 / CAPITAL MARKET
 
▸ MSCI ends 3-year pause on Bangladesh index reviews, to resume in Nov
THE BUSINESS STANDARD
MSCI's August 2026 Equity Index Review said it will resume regular Bangladesh index reviews and corporate-event implementation from November, ending a 3-year "special treatment" pause imposed in 2023 over the floor-price mechanism; the MSCI Bangladesh IMI currently holds 36 stocks.
▸ City Group plans to raise Tk 1,500cr from stock market
THE DAILY STAR
City Group -- one of Bangladesh's largest commodity importers, carrying about Tk25,000cr of outstanding bank debt -- plans to raise up to Tk1,500cr via IPO, private equity, bonds or Sukuk, its first-ever equity-market entry; LankaBangla Investments is issue manager, ONE Bank the banking partner.
■ 03 / MACRO ECONOMY
 
▸ Bangladesh's gross forex reserves rise to $37.11bn
BSS
Bangladesh Bank data showed gross forex reserves rose to $37.11bn as of 13 Aug (IMF BPM6 method: $32.31bn), extending August's climb on strong remittance inflows.
■ 04 / MONEY MARKET & POLICY
 
▸ From floor to runway
THE DAILY STAR
Analysis of Bangladesh Bank's 1 July circular flags an unusual regulatory shift: rather than capping merchant discount rates, BB set a 1% minimum MDR floor for Bangla QR merchant payments, protecting payment-service margins rather than merchants.
■ 05 / REGULATORY
 
▸ BSEC to probe allegations of irregularities against DSE top officials
THE BUSINESS STANDARD
BSEC's Market Intelligence and Investigation Division opened a 30-working-day enquiry (order issued 10 Aug) into DSE's COO, GMs and other senior officials over alleged financial mismanagement, governance failures, irregular recruitment and personal securities trading, following a complaint from shareholder member Don Securities.
▸ BSEC eases mutual fund custodian rules for foreign banks
THE BUSINESS STANDARD MUTUALFUND
BSEC relaxed custodian eligibility requirements for mutual funds, easing the rules under which foreign banks can act as custodian for domestically registered funds.
▸ BSEC rejects Brain Station 23's bid to convert SME listing into IPO
THE BUSINESS STANDARD IT
BSEC rejected IT firm Brain Station 23's application to convert its SME-board listing into a main-board IPO.
