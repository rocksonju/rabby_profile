■ DSE UPDATE
 
DSE held a special trading session on Saturday, 17 October, with the DSEX easing to 5,433.18, down 18.88 points (-0.35%) from Thursday's close. Turnover nearly halved to BDT 317.4cr on 386 scrips traded, from BDT 499.7cr previously. DS30 -0.29% · DSES -0.52%. Market breadth was negative: 96 advanced, 229 declined, 61 unchanged; market PE eased to 8.63x from 8.71x. YTD the DSEX has returned 11.67% (base 4,865.34, 30 Dec 2025). Today's (Sunday) session was pre-open at the time of this report.
