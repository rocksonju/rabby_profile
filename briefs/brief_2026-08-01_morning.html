▸ BSEC, SME Foundation hold training on capital raising for SME entrepreneurs

■ 05 / REGULATORY
 
▸ 5 merged Shariah banks: BB allows up to Tk10 lakh emergency withdrawals
DHAKA TRIBUNE · BANK
BB widened emergency-withdrawal limits for depositors of the five merged Shariah banks to Tk10 lakh, now covering education and other urgent needs beyond medical treatment.
▸ BB consolidates export foreign exchange rules to simplify trade, boost digital transactions
BSS
New consolidated circular FEPD-1 No. 26 updates export FX rules, pushing digitalisation via the Online Export Monitoring System with fresh guidelines for freelancers/ICT exporters and a paperless-trade-document pilot.
▸ BB introduces monthly reporting for private sector foreign debt
BSS
Bangladesh Bank shifted private-sector foreign-debt reporting from quarterly to monthly, effective July 2026 — AD banks must file via the e-returns portal by the 15th of each month to sharpen external-debt oversight.
▸ BB extends deadline for card-to-MFS 'Add Money' compliance until Dec 31
BSS
Bangladesh Bank pushed the card-to-MFS 'Add Money' compliance deadline to 31 Dec 2026; non-compliant banks/MFS providers face service suspension from 1 Jan 2027.

▸ BB launches Tk 2,000cr pre-finance scheme for leather sector development
BSS · LEATHER
BB set up a Tk2,000cr scheme at 7% interest (terms up to 7 years) for leather-sector factory upgrades and environmental compliance, requiring green certification within two years.
